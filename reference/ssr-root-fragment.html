Born in the heart of Kalimantan.

Weekly trails across Samarinda and East Kalimantan nature — then a down-down circle full of cheers. A drinking club with a running problem, since the late ’80s.

SH3 is Samarinda’s inclusive Hash House Harriers chapter — trail, friendship, and circle culture for beginners and old hands. Trails change every week; the spirit does not.

The circle code

No race clock. No strangers for long. Just a few ways we keep the SH3 spirit muddy, merry, and open to everyone.

  1. Follow the flour

    Follow the trail marks, not a finish time. Take in the trees, find your pace, and call On On! to the pack.

  2. Meet the pack

    First hash or old hand, you belong on the trail. Say hello, learn a new name, and look out for each other.

  3. Down-down cheers

    The trail ends; the stories keep going. Ayo join circle for laughter, cheers, and the merry tradition after the run.

  4. Bring a friend

    Good Sundays are better shared. Bring a guest, help them find their feet, and give them a reason to come back.
